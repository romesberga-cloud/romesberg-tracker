Getting Claude to Review Your Progress

There are two easy ways to get Claude to review your logs and suggest plan adjustments:

Option 1 — Export & Paste
Go to Settings → Export Log → Copy to Clipboard. Then open Claude, paste the text, and say: "Review this and suggest adjustments to our workout plans."
Option 2 — Share the App Link
Copy this app's URL from your browser and paste it into a Claude conversation. Claude can read the live data directly and give real-time suggestions.
